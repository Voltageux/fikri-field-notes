## Start with the fit

I want to use AI to help screen jobs for fit before applying. This is a note about that approach, not a report of applications submitted today. The idea is to compare what a vacancy asks for with my actual experience, then leave room for human review before anything is submitted.

My technical interests include civil and environmental engineering, water resources, hydrology, numerical modelling, geospatial analysis and Python-based workflows. Those interests give the search a direction. But being interested in a subject and having the experience a vacancy asks for are different things. That is the comparison I want this approach to keep clear.

A job description might overlap with those interests. The useful question is how its requirements line up with actual experience. A familiar subject is a starting point for that check, not the whole answer.

## Keep a human review before submission

In the proposed workflow, AI would help compare the vacancy requirements with actual experience. That comparison would be something to review before deciding whether to submit an application.

I want the human review to stay part of the process. The comparison should help with the decision, while leaving the application itself for a separate step after review.

So this is the approach I want to take: read the requirements, compare them with actual experience, and review the fit before applying. It is still a proposed way of searching, with no application result to report here.
