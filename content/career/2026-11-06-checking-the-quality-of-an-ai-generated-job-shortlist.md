## What I want the shortlist to help with

This is a note about a possible job-search workflow. It isn't a record of applications I made today, or a report of a shortlist I've already checked.

I want AI assistance to help compare vacancies with my existing background in water resources and computational research. The purpose would be to look at that connection more clearly, rather than chase unrelated roles just because they appear in a list.

For this planned workflow, the useful question is fairly simple: how do the required skills in a vacancy relate to the background I already have? I'd want that comparison to stay close to what the vacancy actually asks for. A shortlist would be a starting point for checking those details.

## What I would still need to check

The human checks should cover whether the vacancy exists, where the work is based, and what location constraints apply. Those details belong alongside the skills comparison. I wouldn't want the planned review to stop at whether a job title sounds relevant.

I'd also need to check the required skills themselves and whether the application instructions are legitimate. These are separate questions, even when they concern the same vacancy.

So the workflow I'm describing has a limited purpose: AI could help with comparing vacancies, while human checks would cover the vacancy and its instructions. This remains an explanatory note about how I want to approach a shortlist. It doesn't establish that I've found suitable roles or taken any application steps.
