## What would be useful to keep

What could I share about a job-search decision? I'd like to keep the criteria used to compare jobs and lessons about the screening process. Those are the parts I wuold want to track in public.

The website includes a public journal about AI-assisted technical job searching, alongside game development and water engineering projects. For the career entries, this is an explanation of what I would like that record to contain. It isn't a report of a new experiment or a completed search task.

The focus would be on how to compare jobs and what can be learned about screening. I could write about the criteria themselves without turning the entry into a copy of an application. That is the level of detail I'd like to keep: enough to explain the decision, with only information that is safe to share.

## What should stay private

Private recruiter correspondence, application answers, contact details, and personally identifying files should stay out of a public post. I wouldn't want a journal entry to become a place for those materials just because they relate to the same job search.

So the planned record has a fairly simple limit. I would like to share comparison criteria and screening lessons, while keeping the private material out. A public explanation doesn't need every detail behind it. The criteria and lessons are what I would want the entry to carry.
