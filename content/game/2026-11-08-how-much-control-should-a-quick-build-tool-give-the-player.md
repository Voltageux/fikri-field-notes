## Where the preset should stop

How much of a restaurant should a quick-build preset decide? For Crust & Crime, that question sits between making a floor plan convenient to build and letting the player decide how to use the space.

The planned quick-build tooling covers restaurant floor plans, furniture, and functional zones. This is a design question for a future build, with no test results yet.

I think a preset could be a useful starting point. But choosing a preset shouldn't have to mean accepting its whole split between dining, kitchen, storage, and staff spaces. That split is part of the control the player should still have.

So the interface question is fairly concrete: could the player accept a starting layout, then change how much space each function gets? I'd use that as something to test, rather than assume that a convenient preset already gives enough freedom.

## What I'd check next

One idea to try would be showing the space allocation alongside the preset choice. Could that help the player understand what they're accepting before using it? Another question is where the controls for changing those areas should sit: beside the preset, or in a separate step afterward?

I'd also check how furniture choices fit into those changes. If the player changes the dining or kitchen allocation, what should the interface let them decide next?

The decision I'd leave open is how much detail belongs in that first choice. A preset needs to be convenient, but the player still needs a clear way to shape the restaurant around it.
