## What I might compare

I would like to investigate sensitivity to Manning roughness in a future river hydraulic modelling case. This is only a plan for a comparison. There are no completed calculations or numerical findings to describe here.

One possible comparison would be to vary channel and floodplain roughness within documented plausible scenarios. The geometry and boundary conditions would stay consistent across those scenarios. That wuold keep the comparison focused on the roughness assumption being changed.

The word documented matters in this plan. The roughness scenarios would need that basis before I could use them for the proposed comparison. For now, the idea is to outline what might be tested, without suggesting that any particular roughness value has already been chosen or checked.

## What I would like to record

I would like to record which water level, velocity, and inundation outputs might be most sensitive to the roughness assumption. Those are the outputs I would want to look at before interpreting a specific numerical result.

So the planned comparison has two parts: keep geometry and boundary conditions consistent while varying roughness, then record how sensitive those outputs might be. I don't have a result to point to yet, and this note doesn't establish which output would be most sensitive.

That is the question for an eventual modelling case. For now, I would like to keep the proposed comparison clear before putting meaning into any numbers.
